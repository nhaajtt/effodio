Glint: describe it, find it

Glint finds the files on your computer by what's in them. Describe it the way you remember it and get the photo, the PDF page, the second of the video. One small model, running on your own PC, in 100+ languages.

Free, for Windows 10 and 11. Nothing you index leaves your computer. It goes online once, to download the model.

Accents optional

"hoa don tien dien" finds "Hóa đơn tiền điện tháng 10". A map by meaning and a list of near-duplicates come with it.

Private · nothing leaves your computer

It only reads your files: never changes, moves or uploads them. It skips keys, certificates and password files, hidden and system folders, and in a code project it reads only screenshots.

PS> powershell -c "irm https://glint.nhaajt.com/install.ps1 | iex"
